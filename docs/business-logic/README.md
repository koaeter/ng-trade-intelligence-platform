# Business Logic

Business logic represents deterministic trade-domain rules and workflows.

Examples:

- Determine applicable export requirements from product, origin, destination, exporter context, and effective date.
- Determine whether an agreement or preferential treatment may apply.
- Determine which agency requirements are relevant.
- Identify requirements affected by a regulatory amendment.
- Combine Nigerian export requirements with destination-market requirements.
- Calculate or retrieve applicable tariff information from authoritative data sources.

Business logic should remain distinct from the AI reasoning layer.

## AI vs business logic

**Business/domain logic:** deterministic rules, validations, calculations, workflows, and policy-independent system behaviour.

**AI/reasoning layer:** natural-language interpretation, synthesis, retrieval orchestration, explanation, comparison, and assisted reasoning over authoritative evidence.
