# Pass 77 — Nigerian Authority Registry Seed

The authority registry now has a governed seed definition for the initial Nigerian export-compliance authority universe.

The seed is intentionally limited to authorities for which the system has a clear compliance-domain relationship. It is not a claim that the listed bodies are the only Nigerian bodies relevant to every product.

Initial authority candidates:

- NEPC — export promotion / exporter registration
- NCS — customs / export declaration / tariff and customs procedures
- SON — standards / conformity
- NAFDAC — regulated food, drugs, cosmetics, devices, chemicals and related products
- NAQS — plant quarantine / phytosanitary controls
- DVPCS — veterinary controls
- Department of Fisheries — fish and fishery-product health certification
- NiNAS — accreditation infrastructure

The registry stores authority identity separately from the authority's source assignments. A later pass will attach domain/fact-specific precedence rather than assigning a single global rank.
