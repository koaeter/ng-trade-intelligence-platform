# Project Vision

## Vision

Build a Nigerian trade intelligence platform that can connect regulatory knowledge with international trade intelligence and provide evidence-grounded answers and analysis.

## Problem

Trade information is distributed across legislation, regulations, government agencies, agreements, international instruments, standards, market-access requirements, and trade-data systems.

The platform should provide a unified way to discover and reason across these sources without treating different instruments or data sources as legally or factually equivalent.

## Intended outcome

For a given product, origin, destination, exporter context, and time period, the platform should be able to assemble the relevant regulatory and market-access landscape and present the supporting evidence.

## Design principles

- Source-first
- Evidence and citation
- Version awareness
- Temporal validity
- Separation of legal instruments
- Deterministic domain rules where possible
- AI-assisted interpretation rather than AI as authority
- Modular architecture
- Auditability
- Extensibility
